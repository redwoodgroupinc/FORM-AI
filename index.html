How it works

Press start. She does the rest.

No wearables, no menus, no guesswork. Just the camera you already carry.

  1. 01

    Prop up your phone

    Lean it against a water bottle 2–3 m away so your whole body is in frame, and press start. There's nothing to choose.

  2. 02

    Just start moving

    She tracks 33 points on your body on-device and recognises squats, push-ups, lunges, curls or presses from your very first rep.

  3. 03

    Listen to her

    She counts your reps out loud and gives short cues like “Chest up” or “Push your knees out” the moment they matter. No need to look at the screen.

Form checks

It sees what you can't see mid-set.

Each check is a measurable joint angle or alignment, tuned per exercise and confirmed over several frames before you hear about it.

Squat

Knees caving in

“Push your knees out”

Compares the gap between your knees to the gap between your ankles at the bottom of each squat.

Squat

Back rounding

“Chest up. Keep your back flat”

Tracks your torso angle against vertical so you hear about a collapsing chest before it costs you the rep.

Push-up

Hips sagging

“Squeeze your glutes. Don't let your hips sag”

Measures how far your hips drop below the line from shoulder to ankle. It also catches hips piking up.

Bicep curl

Elbows drifting

“Pin your elbows to your sides”

Watches your upper arm angle so the biceps do the work instead of your front delts.

Every exercise

Partial reps

“Sit deeper. Hips down to knee level”

A rep only counts when you reach full range. Close-but-not-quite reps get a cue instead of a number.

Every exercise

Rushing reps

“Slow down. Control every rep”

Times each rep from start to finish and flags reps that are too fast to be controlled.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I have to choose an exercise?

No. Press start and begin moving. She recognises squats, push-ups, lunges, bicep curls and overhead presses from your first rep, counts it, and keeps coaching as you switch between them. Each exercise becomes its own set.

Do I need any equipment?

Just a phone or laptop with a camera. Prop it somewhere stable, like against a water bottle or on a shelf, so your whole body is in frame.

Is my video uploaded anywhere?

No. Pose estimation runs on your device. Each frame becomes 33 body coordinates in memory, and only those numbers are used to count reps and check form. Nothing is recorded or sent to a server.

Where should I put my phone?

About 2–3 metres away, around hip height. Squats work best at a 45° angle so both depth and knee tracking are visible. Push-ups and lunges work best side-on. Curls and presses work best facing the camera. If she can't see what she needs, she'll tell you.

How accurate is the rep counting?

Reps are counted from joint angles, so a rep only counts once you reach full range and return to the start. Accuracy is best with your full body in frame, decent lighting and fitted clothing. Baggy clothes and very dark rooms make tracking harder.

Can I change her voice?

She automatically uses the best female voice your phone or browser offers. You can pick a different one, or turn voice off, in the coach's settings.

Does it work offline?

The mobile apps bundle the pose model, so they work fully offline. The web version downloads the model once (about 6 MB) and your browser caches it for next time.

Is this a replacement for a coach or physiotherapist?

No. Form AI Coach gives general technique feedback to help you train more consistently. It is not medical advice. If something hurts, stop and talk to a qualified professional.
