Compatible devices

Runs on the phone or laptop you already have.

There's no app to install. The coach runs in your browser, on your device. Here's what works best, and a live check for the device you're on right now.

iPhone & iPad

iOS / iPadOS 17 or later · Safari, Chrome, Edge or Firefox

Best
iPhone 13 and newer, incl. SE (3rd gen), 16e, iPhone Air and 17e · iPad Pro (M1 or newer) · iPad Air (5th gen) and newer · iPad mini (6th gen) and newer · iPad (A16)
Works
iPhone XS, XR, 11, 12 and SE (2nd gen) · iPad (8th–10th gen) · iPad Air (3rd/4th gen) · iPad mini (5th gen) · iPad Pro 11-inch (2018/2020) and 12.9-inch (2018/2020)
Avoid
iPhone 8, 8 Plus and X and older (they can't update past iOS 16) · iPad (7th gen) and older

Android phones & tablets

Android 10 or later · up-to-date Chrome (Samsung Internet and Edge also work)

Best
Google Pixel 6 to Pixel 9 series (incl. 6a, 7a, 8a) · Samsung Galaxy S21 to S24 series · phones with Snapdragon 888 / 8 Gen 1–3 or Google Tensor
Works
Galaxy A54, A55, A56 and similar 2022+ upper mid-range phones (Snapdragon 778G or 7-series Gen 1+, Exynos 1380+, Dimensity 1080 / 7050+) · other Android 10+ phones with 4 GB+ RAM, more slowly
Note
Galaxy S25, OnePlus 13, Xiaomi 15 and other Snapdragon 8 Elite phones: a Google pose-engine bug affects their GPU, so the coach switches to Compatibility mode automatically. It works, a little slower.

Laptops & desktops

Windows 10/11 · macOS 13 Ventura or later · Chromebook · 64-bit Linux · any webcam

Best
Macs with Apple silicon (M1 or newer) · Intel Core i5 (11th gen) / AMD Ryzen 5 (5000) and newer · any laptop with a graphics card · Chromebook Plus
Works
Intel Macs on macOS 13 or later · Intel Core i3/i5 (8th gen) or AMD Ryzen 3 (3000) and newer with 8 GB RAM, and graphics acceleration turned on in the browser
Avoid
Intel Celeron, Pentium Silver and Atom laptops · entry-level Chromebooks · virtual machines and remote desktops without a GPU

Minimum browser versions

  • Safari 17+
  • Chrome 108+
  • Edge 108+
  • Samsung Internet 21+
  • Firefox 101+

Chrome and Safari work best. Google's pose engine doesn't officially support Firefox: it usually works, but switch to Chrome if tracking misbehaves.

  • Opened the link in Instagram, Facebook, TikTok or Snapchat? Tap “Open in browser”. In-app browsers usually block the camera and her voice.
  • iPhone with Lockdown Mode on? It turns off the graphics the coach needs. Add this site as an exception.
  • Her voice starts after you tap Start. Can't hear her? Check the volume and the ring/silent switch. On Linux, install speech-dispatcher.
  • The first visit downloads about 17 MB, once. After that your browser caches it.
  • We can't test every model. The live check tests the exact device you're using.

    How it works

    Press start. She does the rest.

    No wearables, no menus, no guesswork. Just the camera you already carry.

    1. 01

      Prop up your phone

      Lean it against a water bottle 2–3 m away so your whole body is in frame, and press start. There's nothing to choose.

    2. 02

      Just start moving

      She tracks 33 points on your body on-device and recognises squats, push-ups, lunges, curls or presses from your very first rep.

    3. 03

      Listen to her

      She counts your reps out loud and gives short cues like “Chest up” or “Push your knees out” the moment they matter. No need to look at the screen.

    Form checks

    It sees what you can't see mid-set.

    Each check is a measurable joint angle or alignment, tuned per exercise and confirmed over several frames before you hear about it.

    Squat

    Knees caving in

    “Push your knees out”

    Compares the gap between your knees to the gap between your ankles at the bottom of each squat.

    Squat

    Back rounding

    “Chest up. Keep your back flat”

    Tracks your torso angle against vertical so you hear about a collapsing chest before it costs you the rep.

    Push-up

    Hips sagging

    “Squeeze your glutes. Don't let your hips sag”

    Measures how far your hips drop below the line from shoulder to ankle. It also catches hips piking up.

    Bicep curl

    Elbows drifting

    “Pin your elbows to your sides”

    Watches your upper arm angle so the biceps do the work instead of your front delts.

    Every exercise

    Partial reps

    A rep only counts when you reach full range. Close-but-not-quite reps get a cue instead of a number.

    Every exercise

    Rushing reps

    Times each rep from start to finish and flags reps that are too fast to be controlled.

    FAQ

    Questions, answered.

    Do I have to choose an exercise?

    No. Press start and begin moving. She recognises squats, push-ups, lunges, bicep curls and overhead presses from your first rep, counts it, and keeps coaching as you switch between them. Each exercise becomes its own set.

    Do I need any equipment?

    Just a phone or laptop with a camera. See compatible devices for the models that work best. Prop it somewhere stable, like against a water bottle or on a shelf, so your whole body is in frame.

    Is my video uploaded anywhere?

    No. Pose estimation runs on your device. Each frame becomes 33 body coordinates in memory, and only those numbers are used to count reps and check form. Nothing is recorded or sent to a server.

    Where should I put my phone?

    About 2–3 metres away, around hip height. Squats work best at a 45° angle so both depth and knee tracking are visible. Push-ups and lunges work best side-on. Curls and presses work best facing the camera. If she can't see what she needs, she'll tell you.

    How accurate is the rep counting?

    Reps are counted from joint angles, so a rep only counts once you reach full range and return to the start. Accuracy is best with your full body in frame, decent lighting and fitted clothing. Baggy clothes and very dark rooms make tracking harder.

    Can I change her voice?

    She automatically uses the best female voice your phone or browser offers. You can pick a different one, or turn voice off, in the coach's settings.

    Does it work offline?

    The mobile apps bundle the pose model, so they work fully offline. The web version downloads the pose engine and model once (about 17 MB) and your browser caches them for next time.

    Is this a replacement for a coach or physiotherapist?

    No. Form AI Coach gives general technique feedback to help you train more consistently. It is not medical advice. If something hurts, stop and talk to a qualified professional.
