Privacy policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

ShapeCoach is an AI personal trainer that watches you exercise through your camera, counts your reps and gives you form cues. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the ShapeCoach website or the ShapeCoach Android app. The short version: your camera video never leaves your device, you don't need an account, and we don't track you.

Your camera

ShapeCoach asks for camera access only when you press Start coaching. Each camera frame is analysed in memory on your device by an on-device pose model (Google MediaPipe), turned into body-joint positions and then discarded. Video and images are never recorded, stored or uploaded, and nobody, including us, can see them. You can also analyse a video file from your device; it is processed the same way and is not uploaded.

Information stored on your device

Your settings (for example voice on or off) and a short history of your recent sets (exercise, reps, form score and time) are saved in the app's storage on your device so you can see them next time. They are never sent to us. You can delete your set history with Clear in the coach, and remove everything by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it.

No account, no ads, no tracking

ShapeCoach has no user accounts. We do not use analytics, advertising, tracking cookies or third-party trackers, and we do not sell or share personal information.

Services that deliver the app

Like any website, loading ShapeCoach makes requests to the services that host it. These providers receive your IP address and standard technical information (such as browser type) in order to deliver the files, under their own privacy policies:

  • Vercel, which hosts the ShapeCoach pages.
  • jsDelivr and unpkg, which deliver the pose engine (MediaPipe) software.
  • Google Cloud Storage, which delivers the pose model file.
  • Google Fonts, which delivers the fonts on the website.

Voice cues

Spoken cues use the text-to-speech voices built into your phone or browser. Most voices run on your device. Some browsers offer voices that are generated online by the browser maker (for example Microsoft's "Online" voices in Edge). If one of those is selected, the short cue text (such as "Push your knees out") is processed by that provider. No camera data is ever included.

Subscriptions and payments

ShapeCoach Premium is sold through Google Play. Google processes your payment under the Google Privacy Policy; we never receive your card or payment details. The app only asks Google Play whether your subscription is active, so it can unlock Premium on your device.

Children

ShapeCoach is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Health information

ShapeCoach gives general fitness guidance, not medical advice. The joint positions it computes stay on your device and are not used to diagnose or treat any condition.

Your choices and rights

Because we do not collect personal information on our servers, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on your behalf. All ShapeCoach data lives on your device and you control it as described above. You can turn camera permission off at any time in your phone's settings.

Changes to this policy

If we change how ShapeCoach handles information, we will update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Contact us using the developer email shown on the ShapeCoach listing in Google Play.
